# Start here

Claude runs your job search: it finds and scores postings, writes and checks a resume and
cover letter for the ones you pick, and helps you reach the right people. It never sends
anything; you do. You need a GitHub account (free) and a paid Claude plan. No coding, and
nothing to install first: a phone is enough. Another agent tool? See [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).

## 1. Make your own private copy

<!-- claude-surface: make-copy -->
<!-- checked 2026-10-25;
https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/creating-and-managing-repositories/creating-a-new-repository
-->
To make your own private copy:
1. Open this link, signed in to GitHub:
   https://github.com/new?template_owner=aranculleton&template_name=last-job-search&owner=%40me&name=my-job-search&visibility=private
   A phone's web browser works too.
2. It opens with the job search as the template and Private chosen. Check Private, then
   choose Create repository.
3. If the form opens empty: on https://github.com/aranculleton/last-job-search, choose Use
   this template, then Create a new repository, choose Private, then Create repository from
   template.
4. Never choose Fork: a fork is public, and GitHub never lets one be made private.

Screens change: if yours looks different, tell Claude what you see.

## 2. Let Claude see it

<!-- claude-surface: connect-github -->
<!-- checked 2026-11-03; https://code.claude.com/docs/en/web-quickstart -->
To let Claude see your copy:
1. Open https://claude.ai/code and sign in. When it asks to connect GitHub, follow it and
   approve.
2. Open https://github.com/apps/claude/installations/new, choose your account, then Only
   select repositories, pick your copy, and install.
3. Back at https://claude.ai/code (or the Code tab in the Claude app), choose your copy
   under the message box.

Screens change: if yours looks different, tell Claude what you see.
<!-- /claude-surface -->

## 3. Say "set me up"

Claude makes your private page and sends its link: six short questions are there, one at a
time. Then it does the research and brings your first shortlist: up to an hour, and you can put
it down meanwhile. No resume? Choose "Build one with me".

So Claude carries on while you're away (recommended, once):

<!-- claude-surface: cloud-auto -->

To let Claude carry on while you're away:
1. In your job search chat, open the mode menu in the row under the message box (in the
   Claude app on a phone: the + in the message box, then Permission).
2. Choose Auto. If Auto is not there, your plan or organization does not offer it: Claude
   then asks before some steps, so answer in the chat when it does.

## On your computer

For networking, once: install the Claude desktop app (https://claude.ai/download), open your page's
link in it, and allow Claude's browser when it asks. It reads LinkedIn there by itself and
asks you to sign in to LinkedIn if you are not. Nothing else to install: everything else
runs in the cloud.

## Then use your page

Everything is on it. Tap **Find jobs**, **Draft this** on a job you like, **Find people**,
and **I heard back** when you do; tweak or trash anything that's off, and it remembers. Anything
else, ask in **Chat** there. Updates arrive by themselves; nothing of yours is sent back.

If something goes wrong, see [HELP.md](HELP.md), or tell Claude what you see.

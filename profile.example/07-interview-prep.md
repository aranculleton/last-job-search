## STAR: the forecast that freed safety stock
- **Situation:** forecast error at 22% across 2,400 SKUs and six distribution centres.
- **Task:** a weekly demand forecast the planners would trust.
- **Action:** rebuilt it in Python and SQL with promotions and seasonality as features.
- **Result:** error down to 14%, about £1.1M of safety stock released.
